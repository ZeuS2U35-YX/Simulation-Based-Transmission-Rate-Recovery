Mean-error distributions across recovery periods for three transmission-rate representations. Histograms show mean estimation error in B (estimated minus true B; week⁻¹) across n = 200 matched simulation replicates. Columns show the overall period, through week 5 (week ≤ 5), and after week 5 (week > 5); rows show the Gamma-noise, B-spline and Constant-B models. All panels use a common bin width of 0.10 and a common x-axis. The grey dashed line marks zero error, and the model-coloured dotted line marks the panel mean. No simulation replicates were excluded.
